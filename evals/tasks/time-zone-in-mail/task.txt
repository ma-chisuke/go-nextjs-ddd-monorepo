The mail that confirms a reservation shows its start and its end in UTC. Our rooms are in Tokyo, and people read "01:00 UTC" as the time of the meeting, and come nine hours early or late.

The mail should show the times on the clock of the room, with the name of its time zone, such as `Asia/Tokyo`. For that, the event `reservation.ReservationConfirmed.v1` gets an optional property `timeZone`: the IANA name of the time zone of the room. Reservation knows the room when it books a room and when it reschedules a reservation, and both publish the event.

The change must be compatible: the event stays at version 1, and a consumer that does not know the property goes on working. The events that were published before the change lack the property, and Notification may still receive one of them: for such an event, the mail stays as it is today, in UTC.

Finish the change so that it is ready to be merged.
